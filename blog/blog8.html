Best PR Practices when Using AI Heavily

You might have just graduated, and, like me, have stepped blindly into the workforce knowing very little about the best practices for pull requests. Currently, I am on my second sprint at my new job. The first sprint was truly a warmup round for me as I was heavily relying on the machinations of vibe coding in order to complete my ticket, and fell into the approach every developer dreads: the candle and prayer approach. As a result of letting AI modify whatever it wanted to resolve my query, I ended up stalling team operations and making the PR extremely bulky.

Simple and Straightforward

The number of files modified/created should be no more than 7-8. The people reviewing the PR will ask you a ton of questions that you may not be prepared to explain. I have, in my current sprint, been practicing this workflow, and it has led to a massive decrease in the number of questions asked about my work.

Well Documented

Make sure that other people can easily follow along with the changes you are adding to main and can debug without consulting you.
